How a customer books

  1. Open the salon's public link, e.g. /book/glow-studio
  2. Pick services, staff (or "Any available") and a date
  3. Choose a free slot, enter name + 10-digit phone, confirm
  4. Keep the booking ID — it works with the phone number to cancel or reschedule (not within 2 hours of start)
